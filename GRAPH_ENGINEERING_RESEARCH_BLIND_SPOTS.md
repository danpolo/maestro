This file records unresolved evidence, not additional implementation commitments. No new external research was conducted. The supplied reports’ numerical claims, product versions, and license assertions have not been independently verified.

## Third category — implementation-time external verification (B06, B17)

These two are not research questions and are deliberately **not** closed. Both are facts about
one exact version that expire silently: the local pass observed the installed CLIs having already
left the supplied research behind on three separate points (Codex needs no PTY quota collector,
Antigravity has no usage surface, Claude's reset epoch is null). Researching them now would buy a
stale answer.

They are instead **mandatory integration gates**, mechanised on 2026-09-08 rather than left as a
prose rule — see `docs/DESIGN.md` §15:

> **Integration verification gate.** Any new or materially updated backend, or any optional
> external dependency, must pass implementation-time capability, version, licensing, cost and
> conformance verification before it is marked supported. Verification is recorded against the
> exact installed version, with its evidence, in `maestro/thirdparty.json`.

| Enforcement | Where | Catches |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage | `tests/test_thirdparty.py` | A registered backend or declared distribution with no verification row. Repository-only, no host access. |
| Schema | `maestro/thirdparty.py` | A version claim with no evidence; a dependency with no licence source; a backend row that does not list the controls observed. |
| Drift (soft) | `maestro doctor` | An installed version that moved away from the verified one. Warns; never fails the whole checklist for an overnight CLI upgrade. |
| Drift (gate) | `maestro doctor --thirdparty` | The same, as a required check with a non-zero exit. Run this before declaring an adapter or dependency integration complete. |

A row blocks only when the subject is actually in use — a driver is registered for it, or
`pyproject.toml` declares it — which is what lets the register carry a shortlisted-but-unadopted
provider (Tree-sitter) inertly until adoption puts it in use.

The main plan makes operational choices that remain usable while these questions are open. Experiments E0–E6 in that plan resolve only the questions needed for a proposed promotion; they do not authorize another broad research pass.
